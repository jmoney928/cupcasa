Why PHA

The Next Generation of Compostable

PHA (polyhydroxyalkanoates) is produced by bacteria from plant sugars. It breaks down in your backyard compost — no industrial facility required.

Planet
Home Compostable

Fully breaks down in 6–12 months in backyard compost and soil. Marine biodegradable. No landfill residue.

Health
Zero Microplastics

PHA biodegrades completely — it never fragments into microplastics. No BPA, no PFAS, no phthalates. FDA food contact approved.

Brand
Genuinely Sustainable

Not "compostable in theory" — compostable in reality. A claim your customers can verify at home, not just on a label.
